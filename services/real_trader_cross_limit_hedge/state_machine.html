real_trader_cross_limit_hedge — State Machine 시각화

전략 요약: 15분봉 시작가 crossing 기반, 캔들당 최대 10회 진입. 1회차 UNIT(10), 2~9회차 2*UNIT(20), 10회차 UNIT(10). GTC 고정가 0.70. BTC only.

1. Candle State Machine

캔들(15분)마다 생성되는 per-candle 상태 — CrossingStrategy5M.candle_state

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> IDLE: candle_boundary (reset)

    IDLE --> IDLE: crossing @ elapsed<150s (MIN 이전 무시)
    IDLE --> IDLE: crossing @ elapsed≥900s (CUTOFF 이후 무시)
    IDLE --> ACTIVE_1: crossing @ 150s≤elapsed<870s, count=1, bet UNIT(10)
    IDLE --> FINAL: crossing @ 870s≤elapsed<900s, count=10, bet UNIT(10)

    ACTIVE_1 --> ACTIVE_MID: crossing, count=2, bet 2*UNIT(20)
    ACTIVE_MID --> ACTIVE_MID: crossing, count=3..9, bet 2*UNIT(20)
    ACTIVE_MID --> FINAL: crossing & (count=10 OR elapsed≥870s), bet UNIT(10)
    ACTIVE_1 --> FINAL: crossing @ elapsed≥870s, bet UNIT(10)

    ACTIVE_1 --> FAILED: order rejection (mark_candle_failed)
    ACTIVE_MID --> FAILED: order rejection
    FINAL --> [*]: further crossings ignored (count≥MAX)
    FAILED --> [*]: further crossings ignored
상수: CROSSING_MIN_ELAPSED=150s · CROSSING_CUTOFF=900s · CROSSING_LATE_ENTRY=870s · CROSSING_MAX_COUNT=10

2. Trade Status Machine

개별 주문 1건의 라이프사이클 — test_paper_trades.status

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> PENDING: post_order success & filled=0 (maker 대기)
    [*] --> FILLED: post_order success & takingAmount>0 (즉시 체결)
    [*] --> FAILED: post_order rejected / exception

    PENDING --> FILLED: _check_pending_orders, size_matched>0
    PENDING --> EXPIRED: _check_pending_orders, size_matched=0 (candle_end+10s)
    PENDING --> EXPIRED: get_order 조회 실패

    FILLED --> CLOSED_WIN: settle_trades, side == market_result
    FILLED --> CLOSED_LOSS: settle_trades, side != market_result
    FILLED --> CLOSED_FLAT: settle_trades, market_result=FLAT
    FAILED --> CLOSED_FAILED: settle_trades (pnl=0)

    EXPIRED --> [*]
    CLOSED_WIN --> [*]
    CLOSED_LOSS --> [*]
    CLOSED_FLAT --> [*]
    CLOSED_FAILED --> [*]
